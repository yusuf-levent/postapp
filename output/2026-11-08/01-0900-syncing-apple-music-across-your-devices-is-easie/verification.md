- **Karar:** ✅ `supported`
- **Kanıtlar:**
  - [https://www.engadget.com/2307469/how-to-sync-apple-music-across-devices/](https://www.engadget.com/2307469/how-to-sync-apple-music-across-devices/)
    > You can, for example, sign in with your Apple Account and listen to your favorite songs on an Android phone, Windows PC or even smart TVs.

### 2. Apple Music uygulamasında hesap kontrolü için Ana sekmeden profil resmine dokunulması gerekmektedir.
- **Karar:** ✅ `supported`
- **Kanıtlar:**
  - [https://www.engadget.com/2307469/how-to-sync-apple-music-across-devices/](https://www.engadget.com/2307469/how-to-sync-apple-music-across-devices/)
    > If you're not sure which account you're using, open the Apple Music app and tap your profile picture on the Home tab.

### 3. iPhone veya iPad'de Sync Library özelliğini etkinleştirmek için Ayarlar içerisindeki Uygulamalar bölümünden Müzik uygulamasının bulunması gerekir.
- **Karar:** ✅ `supported`
- **Kanıtlar:**
  - [https://www.engadget.com/2307469/how-to-sync-apple-music-across-devices/](https://www.engadget.com/2307469/how-to-sync-apple-music-across-devices/)
    > On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings and look for Apps. Then, find the Music app in the list and enable Sync Library.

### 4. Mac'te Sync Library özelliğini aktif hale getirmek için Müzik uygulamasındaki Ayarlar altından Genel sekmesine gidilmelidir.
- **Karar:** ✅ `supported`
- **Kanıtlar:**
  - [https://www.engadget.com/2307469/how-to-sync-apple-music-across-devices/](https://www.engadget.com/2307469/how-to-sync-apple-music-across-devices/)
    > On a Mac, open the Music app and click the "Music" menu at the top of the screen, then go to Settings. Now, under the General tab, make sure Sync Library is enabled and click OK to save your settings.

### 5. Android telefonlarda Sync Library seçeneği varsayılan olarak etkin gelmektedir.
- **Karar:** ✅ `supported`
- **Kanıtlar:**
  - [https://www.engadget.com/2307469/how-to-sync-apple-music-across-devices/](https://www.engadget.com/2307469/how-to-sync-apple-music-across-devices/)
    > If you have an Android phone, this option is enabled by default, and you don't need to change anything.

### 6. Mac uygulamasında bulut kütüphanesini güncellemeye zorlamak için Dosya menüsünden Kütüphane ve ardından Update Cloud Library seçeneği seçilmelidir.
- **Karar:** ✅ `supported`
- **Kanıtlar:**
  - [https://www.engadget.com/2307469/how-to-sync-apple-music-across-devices/](https://www.engadget.com/2307469/how-to-sync-apple-music-across-devices/)
    > In the Mac app, click the File menu, then go to Library and select Update Cloud Library — this will force the app to sync and check for updates in your personal library.

### 7. Android ve iOS uygulamalarında Update Cloud Library seçeneği bulunmamaktadır.
- **Karar:** ✅ `supported`
- **Kanıtlar:**
  - [https://www.engadget.com/2307469/how-to-sync-apple-music-across-devices/](https://www.engadget.com/2307469/how-to-sync-apple-music-across-devices/)
    > Unfortunately, the Android and iOS apps don't have that option.

### 8. Apple Music aboneliği olmayan ve Mac veya PC'de çevrimdışı kütüphanesi olan kullanıcılar, iTunes veya Apple Devices aracılığıyla Müzik klasörünü manuel olarak aktarabilirler.
- **Karar:** ✅ `supported`
- **Kanıtlar:**
  - [https://www.engadget.com/2307469/how-to-sync-apple-music-across-devices/](https://www.engadget.com/2307469/how-to-sync-apple-music-across-devices/)
    > If you don't subscribe to Apple Music and have an offline library on your Mac or PC, the only way to sync your library is to manually transfer the Music folder to another computer via Apple iTunes or Apple Devices.
