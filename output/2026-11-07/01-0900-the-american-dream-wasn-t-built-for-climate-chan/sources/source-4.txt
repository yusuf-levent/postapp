We’re providing funding for organizations building AI for teachers.
Google.org has long supported teachers and students, including free AI training for educators. Today, Google.org is providing over $9.5 million in funding across five organizations building with and for educators, aiming to help millions of students reach their full potential:
- The City University of New York: Helping educators build digital and computational literacy through the Computing Integrated Teacher Education (CITE) initiative
- Fab AI: Collaborating with Google DeepMind and the Gates Foundation to build open models that help teachers plan lessons and support early reading
- Krause Center for Innovation: Helping school districts bring responsible AI education into classrooms
- Teaching Lab: Piloting Coherence OS, an operating system that gives teachers and leaders real-time support
- 1EdTech Global: Building public infrastructure for trusted learning standards
