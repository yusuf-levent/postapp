- **Karar:** ✅ `supported`
- **Kanıtlar:**
  - [https://www.engadget.com/2291533/what-is-theater-mode-on-apple-watch/](https://www.engadget.com/2291533/what-is-theater-mode-on-apple-watch/)
    > the original Apple Watch, which first hit stores back in 2015

    > meaning even the original Apple Watch, which first hit stores back in 2015, can utilize it.

### 3. Theater Mode aktif edildiğinde Apple Watch ekranı kararır, cihaz sessize alınır ve görsel bildirimler devre dışı bırakılır.
- **Karar:** ✅ `supported`
- **Kanıtlar:**
  - [https://www.engadget.com/2291533/what-is-theater-mode-on-apple-watch/](https://www.engadget.com/2291533/what-is-theater-mode-on-apple-watch/)
    > Activating Theater Mode will dim the Apple Watch's bright lights and hush it down; it will turn off the display, silence the device and disable visual notifications.

### 4. Theater Mode aktifken bilek kaldırıldığında ekranın uyanması engellenir ve ekranı açmak için ekrana dokunmak veya düğmelere basmak gerekir.
- **Karar:** ✅ `supported`
- **Kanıtlar:**
  - [https://www.engadget.com/2291533/what-is-theater-mode-on-apple-watch/](https://www.engadget.com/2291533/what-is-theater-mode-on-apple-watch/)
    > In addition, it will also keep the display from waking whenever you raise your wrist, meaning you'll need to tap the screen or press either of the Apple Watch's buttons to turn the display back on.

### 5. Theater Mode, Do Not Disturb modundan farklı olarak bildirim titreşimlerini (haptics) açık tutmaya devam eder.
- **Karar:** ✅ `supported`
- **Kanıtlar:**
  - [https://www.engadget.com/2291533/what-is-theater-mode-on-apple-watch/](https://www.engadget.com/2291533/what-is-theater-mode-on-apple-watch/)
    > The big difference between this mode and the total serenity of Do Not Disturb (besides the disabled display) is that Theater Mode will allow you to keep haptics on, meaning you'll still be able to feel the vibration from a notification.

### 6. Theater Mode, Apple Watch'un yan tarafındaki düz oval düğmeye basılarak açılan Kontrol Merkezi üzerinden aktif edilir.
- **Karar:** ✅ `supported`
- **Kanıtlar:**
  - [https://www.engadget.com/2291533/what-is-theater-mode-on-apple-watch/](https://www.engadget.com/2291533/what-is-theater-mode-on-apple-watch/)
    > If you want to turn this feature on, you only need to tap your watch's side button (the flat oval button) to bring up the Control Center. From there, you can toggle Theater Mode by tapping the icon resembling the theatrical comedy-tragedy masks.

### 7. Theater Mode, ekranın sürekli uyanmasını engelleyerek pil tüketimini azaltabilir.
- **Karar:** ✅ `supported`
- **Kanıtlar:**
  - [https://www.engadget.com/2291533/what-is-theater-mode-on-apple-watch/](https://www.engadget.com/2291533/what-is-theater-mode-on-apple-watch/)
    > Chiefly, it can reduce battery drain by stopping your watch from waking up at the slightest gesture.

### 8. Kullanıcılar, Theater Mode aktifken Ayarlar menüsünden kan oksijeni gibi belirli biyometrik ölçümleri devre dışı bırakmayı seçebilir.
- **Karar:** ✅ `supported`
- **Kanıtlar:**
  - [https://www.engadget.com/2291533/what-is-theater-mode-on-apple-watch/](https://www.engadget.com/2291533/what-is-theater-mode-on-apple-watch/)
    > You can also choose to suppress certain biometric readings, such as blood oxygen readings, while your watch is in this mode. To toggle this, you can simply head to Settings and click on the reading you want to keep on or off during Theater Mode, if applicable.
