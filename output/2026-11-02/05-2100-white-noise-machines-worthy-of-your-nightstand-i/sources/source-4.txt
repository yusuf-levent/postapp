See who has access to your Gmail inbox in 2 minutes.
When’s the last time you checked who (or what) has access to your Gmail inbox? Run a quick Google Security Checkup and protect your email with three quick and easy steps:
- Review signed-in devices.
- Remove old third-party app access.
- Confirm recovery information.
For more ways to keep your inbox safe from phishing and unwanted senders, check out the Gmail Help Center guide on protecting your account.
