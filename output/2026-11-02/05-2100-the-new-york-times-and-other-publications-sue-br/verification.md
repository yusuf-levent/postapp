    > According to a complaint filed by the companies that run New York Daily News, the Chicago Tribune, and the Orlando Sentinel, Brave Software has monetized its index of scraped websites by, in part, providing AI companies with paid access to “real-time online content from the Brave Index.”

### 3. Brave, Anthropic, Perplexity, Mistral, xAI ve Cohere dahil olmak üzere birçok yapay zeka şirketine çalınan telif haklı içerik sağladı.
