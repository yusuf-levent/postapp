On Friday, October 23, the highly anticipated iPhone Duo made its debut at Apple Store locations worldwide. Across the globe, customers filled stores and team members invited them to experience the first foldable iPhone, offering hands-on demos and personalized shopping support.
Customers can explore and shop for the revolutionary iPhone Duo and the incredible iPhone lineup at Apple Store locations around the world, on apple.com, and in the Apple Store app. Dedicated Apple Specialists are available to provide world-class support, from maximizing value with Apple Trade In and monthly leasing options with Apple Upgrade, to Apple Card Monthly Installments and exceptional carrier deals. Through Personal Setup, customers can safely and securely transfer their data, and with new Today at Apple sessions including “Say Hello to iPhone Duo” and “Tips: What’s New in iOS 27 on iPhone,” they can take their passions further with the products they love.
