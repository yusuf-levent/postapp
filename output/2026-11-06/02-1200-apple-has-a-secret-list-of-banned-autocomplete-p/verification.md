## İddia Bazlı Sonuçlar

### 1. Araştırmacı Derik Kauffman, iOS'ta otomatik tamamlama ve kaydırarak yazma özelliklerinde engellenmiş 100.000'den fazla kelime ve ifade buldu.
- **Karar:** ✅ `supported`
- **Kanıtlar:**
  - [https://www.theverge.com/tech/1028663/apple-ios-keyboard-blocked-words-autocomplete](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1028663/apple-ios-keyboard-blocked-words-autocomplete)
    > A researcher found over 100,000 words and phrases in “every major language” that are blocked from autocomplete and swipe-type in iOS, Reuters reports. The researcher, Derik Kauffman

### 2. iOS klavyesi, engellenen kelimeleri tek tek harf yazarak yazmaya izin verirken, tahmin metni önerilerinde göstermiyor.
- **Karar:** ✅ `supported`
- **Kanıtlar:**
  - [https://www.theverge.com/tech/1028663/apple-ios-keyboard-blocked-words-autocomplete](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1028663/apple-ios-keyboard-blocked-words-autocomplete)
    > The researcher, Derik Kauffman, says you can still type the words on the blocklist one letter at a time. But they won’t pop up as predictive text suggestions

### 3. iOS klavyesinin engellediği ifadeler arasında 'dictator Trump', 'covid is a hoax', 'illegal immigrant', 'sleepy Joe' ve 'left wing conspiracy' gibi siyasi içerikli ifadeler yer alıyor.
- **Karar:** ✅ `supported`
- **Kanıtlar:**
  - [https://www.theverge.com/tech/1028663/apple-ios-keyboard-blocked-words-autocomplete](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1028663/apple-ios-keyboard-blocked-words-autocomplete)
    > But it also contains political phrases like “illegal immigrant,” “dictator Trump,” “sleepy Joe,” “covid was a hoax,” and “left wing conspiracy”

### 4. Derik Kauffman, engellenen kelime listesini ve farklı dillere ait alt listeleri internette yayınladı.
- **Karar:** ✅ `supported`
- **Kanıtlar:**
  - [https://www.theverge.com/tech/1028663/apple-ios-keyboard-blocked-words-autocomplete](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1028663/apple-ios-keyboard-blocked-words-autocomplete)
    > Kauffman posted the full list online, including the individual lists for different languages.

### 5. Derik Kauffman, engellenen kelime listesini bulmak için OpenAI'ın Codex ve Anthropic'in Claude Code araçlarını kullandı.
- **Karar:** ✅ `supported`
- **Kanıtlar:**
  - [https://www.theverge.com/tech/1028663/apple-ios-keyboard-blocked-words-autocomplete](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1028663/apple-ios-keyboard-blocked-words-autocomplete)
    > According to Reuters, Kauffman used OpenAI’s Codex and Anthropic’s Claude Code to help find the list “deep in the bowels” of iOS.

### 6. Kauffman, engellenen kelime listesinin iOS içerisinde bir hash tablosu olarak saklandığını ve bunu metne dönüştürdüğünü belirtti.
- **Karar:** ✅ `supported`
- **Kanıtlar:**
  - [https://www.theverge.com/tech/1028663/apple-ios-keyboard-blocked-words-autocomplete](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1028663/apple-ios-keyboard-blocked-words-autocomplete)
    > On his website, Kauffman says the phrase list was stored as a hash table, which he was able to convert back to text.

### 7. Apple, klavyesinin potansiyel olarak uygunsuz veya saldırgan önerilerden kaçınmak için tasarlandığını ancak kullanıcıların istediklerini yazabildiklerini açıkladı.
- **Karar:** ✅ `supported`
- **Kanıtlar:**
  - [https://www.theverge.com/tech/1028663/apple-ios-keyboard-blocked-words-autocomplete](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1028663/apple-ios-keyboard-blocked-words-autocomplete)
    > Apple told Reuters that its keyboard is designed to avoid potentially inappropriate or offensive suggestions, but iPhone users can still type in what they want, or override predictive text suggestions.

### 8. Başka bir araştırmacı, Reuters'a verdiği demeçte aynı engellenen kelime listesine bağımsız olarak ulaştığını doğruladı.
- **Karar:** ✅ `supported`
- **Kanıtlar:**

    > Another researcher told Reuters they were able to independently obtain the same list.
