AI in the learning era
We’re announcing major updates to our education portfolio: launching Guided Learning into Google Classroom globally, bringing Guided Learning capabilities to Google Search, and updating our approach to controls for Gemini, Gemini Notebook, Gemini in Classroom, and Google Search for K-12 student accounts. We’re also sharing new research, new Google.org funding, and expanding AI training for educators.
